## 4. Getting reviews

You have 4.9 stars from 40 reviews. The main competitors have 122 and 214. Volume and recency both count, so aim for a steady 4–8 new reviews a month rather than one big push.

**Your review link:** https://wlcreationx.co.za/review

It opens Google's "write a review" box for your profile directly. Put it in your email signature, on invoices, and in the message you send when you hand over final files.

**WhatsApp message (send on the day you deliver the final files):**
> Hi {name}, thanks again for working with us on {project}. If you were happy with it, would you mind leaving us a short Google review? It takes about a minute and helps other businesses find us: https://wlcreationx.co.za/review

**Email:**
> Subject: A quick favour, {name}?
>
> Hi {name},
>
> Thank you for trusting us with {project}. If you're happy with how it turned out, a short Google review would mean a lot to a small studio like ours. It takes about a minute:
>
> https://wlcreationx.co.za/review
>
> If anything wasn't right, please reply to this email instead and tell me. I'd much rather fix it.
>
> {your name}, WL CreationX

**Rules (Google's policy, and what keeps reviews safe):**
- Ask every client, not only the happy ones you expect five stars from. Selective asking ("review gating") breaks Google's rules.
- Never offer a discount, gift or anything else in exchange for a review.
- Never write reviews for yourself or ask staff, family or friends to.
- Reply to every review, good or bad, within a few days. Mention the service in your reply ("Thanks for trusting us with your packaging design"), which also helps relevance.
