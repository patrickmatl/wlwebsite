## What Actually Drives Corporate Video Costs?

### Crew size and shoot length

The biggest lever. A half-day, single-camera shoot (our R12,500 brand story tier) roughly doubles in production effort when it becomes a full day with two cameras, lighting and dedicated audio — which is why the full corporate package sits at R21,500.

### Number of locations

Every extra location means travel, setup and teardown time. We film across Pretoria, Centurion and greater Gauteng — including Johannesburg, Sandton and Midrand — at no additional travel cost; shoots further afield are quoted with travel and accommodation listed separately so you can see exactly what you are paying for.

### Drone work

Aerial footage adds from R3,450, and legality matters: aerial filming must be flown by a licensed operator within South African civil aviation rules, and some sites sit in restricted airspace. We check this during pre-production rather than surprising you on the day.

### Editing rounds and versions

Editing is where budgets quietly leak. Our packages include the revision rounds in the price — two on a brand story video, three on the corporate package — and additional deliverables like vertical cutdowns are priced per item (from R1,150) rather than reopening the quote. When comparing quotes, always ask how many revision rounds are included and what each extra version costs.

### Scripting and pre-production

A scripted film with interview direction takes more preparation than run-and-gun event coverage. Scriptwriting and storyboarding adds from R2,400 if it is not already part of your package, and it is money well spent: an approved script before the shoot prevents the far more expensive fix of re-shooting.

## How Long Does a Corporate Video Take?

A typical corporate video runs three to four weeks end to end: about a week of pre-production (script, shot list, scheduling), one shoot day, and roughly two weeks for editing and revisions. We deliver a first cut within five working days of the shoot. Event highlights are faster — a teaser clip can be delivered the next day.

## How Do You Keep the Budget Down Without Killing the Result?

Four honest ways, from our side of the camera:

1. **Combine photography and video on the same day.** The crew, lighting and location are already set up, so adding stills to a video shoot costs far less than a separate [photography booking](/photography-services-pretoria).
2. **Consolidate locations.** Two scenes at one venue cost less than one scene at each of two venues.
3. **Approve the script before the shoot, not after.** Changes on paper are free; changes that require re-shooting are not.
4. **Plan the cutdowns upfront.** Telling us you need vertical social versions before the shoot means we frame for them; deciding afterwards can mean compromised crops.

If video is part of a bigger campaign, our [service bundles](/service-bundles-pretoria) combine video with design, photography and marketing work from one team, which is generally cheaper than briefing four suppliers.
