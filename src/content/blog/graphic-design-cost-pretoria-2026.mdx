## What Actually Drives Graphic Design Prices?

In our experience since 2013, five things move the price more than anything else:

### 1. Number of unique concepts

Every original option is a fresh creative exploration. Two options cost less than six because the design time roughly scales with it.

### 2. Revisions beyond the included rounds

We include two free revision rounds in every price. Extra revisions are R520 each, billed as graphic design time — there is no limit on paid revisions, but a clear brief upfront usually means you never need them.

### 3. Complexity and page count

A single-sided flyer is a different job from a 24-page company profile. That is why multi-page work like company profiles is priced per page (from R780) rather than as a flat fee.

### 4. Custom illustration

A wordmark or lettermark logo is quicker to produce than an illustrative logo with custom drawn artwork — which is why our illustrative brand packages cost roughly R5,300 more than their standard equivalents.

### 5. What is included in the handover

This is the one buyers miss. Our prices include final artwork in open file formats plus your logo colour codes and fonts at handover. Many cheaper quotes exclude native files entirely, which locks you into that supplier for every future change. Always ask.
